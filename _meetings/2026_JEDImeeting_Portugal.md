## About the Meeting

The aim of the fly JEDI meeting is to create, maintain and develop a network of junior researchers using Drosophila as a model organism. The 2026 JEDI Meeting will bring together 50 early-career Drosophila researchers for three days of scientific exchange, collaboration, and community building.

Over the course of three days, all participants will present their research in short scientific talks designed to stimulate focused feedback and spark new collaborations. The scientific program spans a wide range of topics, including cell and developmental biology, neurobiology and behavior, evolution, cancer research, and systems-level approaches.

## Keynote Speaker: Eugenia Chiappe

<img src="/assets/img/JEDI2026/Eugenia.png" alt="Eugenia" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" style="max-width: 200px; cursor: pointer; float: left; margin-right: 15px;" data-zoomable>

We are delighted to welcome **Eugenia Chiappe** as our keynote speaker. Eugenia Chiappe is a principal investigator of neuroscience in the Champalimaud Foundation in Lisbon, Portugal. To ensure the success of the most trivial, or the most elaborate action, the brain of all animals, from insects to humans, has to simultaneously process and combine rapidly changing sensory information, while monitoring the position of all body parts, their speed of movement and the body's posture. Her lab is interested in understanding how the brain orchestrates all these signals to form an internal representation of self-movement, and how this representation controls locomotion.

Her work creatively combines genetics, physiology, behavioural setup development, and modelling to address fundamental questions in neuroscience.

→ [Learn more about her research](https://chiappelab.org/)

### Palmela, Portugal

Palmela is a historic town located approximately 40 minutes south of Lisbon, in the Setúbal district. Surrounded by vineyards and close to the Atlantic coast and Arrábida Natural Park, it provides a quiet and scenic environment ideal for focused scientific exchange.

### Getting There

**Lisbon International Airport (LIS)** connects directly to major European cities. From Lisbon, Palmela is easily accessible by car (~40 minutes) or public transport.

Portugal offers a strong and growing life-science community within a culturally rich and easily accessible European setting, combining mild climate, excellent gastronomy, and a vibrant research environment.
